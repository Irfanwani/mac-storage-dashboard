Grant Full Disk Access

    

      
macOS protects folders like Desktop, Documents, Mail and
      Photos. Your browser can't read your disk — the local scanner does that, and macOS grants disk
      access to the app that launched it:

      
    
        
  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
    2. 
        
  2. Enable your terminal app (click + and add it from Applications if missing).
    3. 
        
  3. Fully quit and reopen that app — macOS applies the permission on restart.
    4. 
        
  4. Come back here and hit Rescan.
    5. 
      

      
No Terminal involved: run
      build_app.command once, then launch Mac Storage Dashboard.app — macOS will ask for access
      on behalf of the app itself instead. (Granting your browser Full Disk Access adds nothing here: browsers
      are sandboxed and never scan your disk.)

      
Without Full Disk Access the dashboard still works — blocked
      folders are simply skipped and shown as under-reported. Nothing leaves this Mac.
